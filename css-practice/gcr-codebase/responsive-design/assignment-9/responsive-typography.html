Learning Web Development in 2024

Web development is one of the most in-demand skills in the world today. Whether you want to build websites for clients or launch your own startup, knowing HTML, CSS and JavaScript will take you a long way. The best part is that you can start learning for free online and practice by building your own projects every day.

Why Responsive Design Matters

More than 60 percent of internet traffic now comes from mobile devices. If your website does not look good on a phone, you are losing more than half your visitors. Responsive design makes sure your website works on all screen sizes. It uses flexible layouts, relative units like rem and vw, and media queries to adjust styles.
