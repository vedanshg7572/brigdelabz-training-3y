History of Jaipur

Jaipur was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II. It was one of the first planned cities of India. The city is called the Pink City because most of its buildings are painted in pink color. Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan and is a major tourist destination in India.

Famous Places

Jaipur is home to many historical monuments. Hawa Mahal is a five-storey palace with 953 windows. Amber Fort is a large fort on a hill outside the city. City Palace is still home to the royal family of Jaipur. Jantar Mantar is an ancient astronomical observatory built in the 18th century.

Famous Food

Jaipur is known for its delicious food. Dal Baati Churma is the most famous dish of Rajasthan. Ghewar is a sweet dish made during festivals. Pyaaz Kachori is a popular street food. Laal Maas is a spicy mutton curry that is unique to Rajasthan.

How to Travel

Jaipur is well connected by road, rail, and air. The Jaipur International Airport connects it to major cities in India and abroad. The city has a good railway network and is connected to Delhi, Mumbai, and other cities. Local transport includes autos, taxis, and the Jaipur Metro.
