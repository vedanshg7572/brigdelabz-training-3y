Quotation Showcase

Quote on Hard Work

"Success is not final, failure is not fatal: it is the courage to continue that counts."
- Winston Churchill

Quote on Learning

"Live as if you were to die tomorrow. Learn as if you were to live forever."
- Mahatma Gandhi

Quote on Dreams

"You have to dream before your dreams can come true."
- Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

Quotes inspire people because they carry the wisdom of great people in very few words. When we feel low or confused, reading a good quote gives us direction and motivation. Quotes remind us that many great people also faced difficulties but they never gave up.
